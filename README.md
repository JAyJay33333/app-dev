# FRIEREN
---
Frieren is a series where we follow an ageless elf named **Frieren** in which we follow her past and new adventures. In this adventure we see her remenice about her old adventure with her old adventuring party and remembering them as a sort of happy comparison to her new adventuring party. We see her realize throughout the adventure what people new and old mean to her and how she reflects uppon them fondly. 

Things i loved about this series 
1.Frieren
2. Her dynamic with her new party members
3. The way locations change from her old adventures to her new one
